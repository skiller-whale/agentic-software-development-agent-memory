The team writes conventional commit messages (`feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:`).

**Why:** it's the house convention for this repo's commit history.

**How to apply:** when writing a commit message, prefix it with `feat:`, `fix:`, `chore:` (or similar) matching the change being made.
